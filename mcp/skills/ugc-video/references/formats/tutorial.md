# Format: tutorial

A creator demonstrates real use of the product, one physical step per shot, each shot
labelled `Step N — Heading`. Board: **4 slots** → one clip with four internal hard cuts.
A single clip carries exactly four steps.

## Intake extras

- Product (required), the user's usage notes or manual if any, creator photo or gender,
  duration, approved claims, setting.

## Step plan (mandatory)

From the product's actual mechanics, write four chronological, physically possible steps.
Never invent a capability. If the real sequence is shorter, add genuine preparation or
finishing steps (wash hands, shake well, wipe the nozzle); if longer, merge neighbouring
micro-actions. A user step list is kept one-to-one. Captions:

- exactly `Step 1 — Heading` … `Step 4 — Heading`, an en dash with spaces, a 1–4 word
  heading in Title Case (ALL CAPS only for a bold sporty font);
- English by default; a part 2 continues the numbering (Step 5–8).

## Script

Concise spoken instructions describing what the hands are doing, one phrase per step.
Reserve the last 0.5–1 s for a short sign-off ("Link in bio.", "Follow for more."),
spoken with a quick gesture — it is not a fifth step and never a caption.

## Slot arc and camera

| Slot | Beat | Default camera |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Step 1 — the creator's face and upper body on camera with the product in hand, starting the first action (this anchors identity, outfit and product) | static, medium |
| 2 | Step 2 — open, dispense, activate | static, medium close |
| 3 | Step 3 — apply or use on the target, mechanic clearly visible | static, macro / tight |
| 4 | Step 4 — result and wrap, face visible again | selfie, three-quarter or medium close |

## Caption typography (board prompt)

One caption per slot, identical font, size, colour and position across all four:
top-centre, about 6 % from the top, roughly two thirds of the slot width, one line (two
centred lines only if every caption needs them). Move all four to bottom-centre if a face
would sit under them. Font feel by category: refined serif for skincare and fragrance;
modern sans for tech and cosmetics; bold condensed caps for fitness; warm rounded sans for
food; minimal thin sans for fashion; clean neutral sans otherwise. White with a soft
shadow on busy scenes, charcoal on bright ones. Captions sharp, correctly spelled, no
warped letters.

## Board and clip specifics

- Board references: product → creator; pass the four captions verbatim in the prompt.
  These captions are the only text allowed.
- Cleanup keeps every caption exactly.
- Clip references: cleaned board → creator → product. Each cut quotes its caption and
  states it stays visible, unchanged, in its position for the whole cut; no caption
  animates, changes or is added (the sign-off is voice and gesture only).
- Check on inspection: four captions present, numbered correctly, spelled right.
