# Format: product (voiceover, no on-camera speaker)

The product is the hero of every shot; an off-screen voice narrates. A person, if
visible at all, is incidental — hands only, cropped at the chest or waist, a first-person
view, or a far figure — never the subject, never speaking. Board: **4 slots** → one clip
with four internal hard cuts.

A silent product commercial (no narration) is not this format — say it is out of scope
for this skill and point to ordinary video generation.

## Intake extras

- A real product reference is required (photo or catalog item). Never invent one.
- Duration; voice gender (default to what fits the product, state it for veto);
  language and accent; approved claims; setting; music.
- No creator lock — skip the creator stage entirely.

## Script

Voiceover only, written to the monologue budgets. Split into four beat-sized phrases,
one per slot. Specific sensory or mechanical lines, no greeting, no unsupported claims.

## Slot arc

| Slot | Beat | Default framing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce — the product at rest in its natural place, not yet in use | static, medium-wide |
| 2 | Demo A — the product doing its job, first angle | static, medium |
| 3 | Demo B — a clearly different action, target or context (not just closer) | macro or first-person |
| 4 | Result — the visible outcome, or a hero shot clearly unlike slot 1 | medium-wide or three-quarter |

Pick Demo A and B as two different things: mouse glide vs scroll-wheel macro; mist on the
wrist vs droplets settling on skin; pour vs sip; garment held up vs worn and smoothed;
vacuum on carpet vs nozzle at a ceiling corner. Every slot covers a different distance
band (tight, medium, wide all present).

Ground the product in its world with two or three everyday accessories spread across
the slots (a vanity with brushes for cosmetics, ingredients on a counter for food, a
desk mat for a mouse, a towel and shaker for supplements).

## Board and clip specifics

- Board references: product (→ previous cleaned board for a part 2). Board prompt says:
  any person is incidental and cropped, mouth closed, gender matching the voice.
- Clip references: cleaned board → product.
- Clip Audio line: "An off-screen [female|male] voice narrates, close and natural, phone-
  mic quality with room tone: "…"". Add to the bans: no one on camera speaks, no
  lip-sync, no face talking to the lens.
- No generated text.
