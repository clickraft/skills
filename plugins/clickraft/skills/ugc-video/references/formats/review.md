# Format: review (default)

One adult creator talks to camera in one 9:16 clip. With a product, they demonstrate it;
without one, they tell a story or share a routine — never invent a product for them.
Board: **8 slots** (4 for clips under 8 s) → one clip with matching internal hard cuts.

## Intake extras

- Creator: photo (with consent), brand model, or gender for a generated creator.
- Product: optional. Never ask for one if none was given.
- Duration (8, 10, 12 or 15 s offered).
- Optional: approved claims, setting, mood, music, accent or quirk (only when the brief
  hints at it), a beat list (director mode).

## Pipeline

1. Product intake if a product exists.
2. Creator lock.
3. Monologue — story mode by default (one scenario, product as a supporting actor around
   the middle); plain review structure only on "just review it".
4. Board with the arc below, reference order product → creator.
5. Cleanup, inspection.
6. Clip prompt: refs cleaned board → creator → product.

## Slot arc (single clip)

| Slot | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook — already mid-moment, using the chosen hook pattern |
| 2 | Context — where we are, what is going on |
| 3 | Life beat — the person, not the product |
| 4 | The turn — first touch of the product, or the story's "but then" |
| 5 | Core action — the product doing its visible thing (or the story's key action) |
| 6 | Reaction — the honest peak, with a body movement |
| 7 | Result — what is visibly different, or the story lands |
| 8 | Settle — warm recommendation or closing thought, loop-ready |

With 4 slots: hook → turn/first touch → core action with peak → settle.

Slot 1 is the opening moment, not automatically a product shot. Default camera cadence
for 8 slots: selfie-medium → static-wide → static-macro → selfie-tight → static-medium →
static-macro → static-wide → selfie-tight. Adapt it to the actions, keeping neighbours
different.

## Format rules

- One creator image for every stage; identical face and outfit across slots.
- No generated text of any kind — no captions, labels or titles in board or clip.
- Productless: no product, package, brand, label or sales call appears anywhere; the
  hands do the story's actions instead.
- Delivery copy calls a product video a brand demo or creator concept, never a customer
  review.
