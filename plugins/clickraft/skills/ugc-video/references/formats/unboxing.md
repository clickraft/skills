# Format: unboxing

A visible creator opens a sealed package and reacts. Use it only when the brief asks for
a creator-led reveal — a product simply coming out of a box with nobody on camera is the
`product` format. Board: **4 slots** → one clip with four internal hard cuts.

## Intake extras

- Product (required), creator photo or gender, duration.
- Ask once whether a real package photo exists. A plain "yes" is not a photo — wait for
  the image. Without one, the box is a plain brown taped delivery box with no logos or
  printed branding.
- Approved claims, language, accent, look or location overrides.

## Script

Hook that fits a reveal (a small confession, a specific anticipation), a body reaction at
the reveal, one turn, a natural close. The first word is content.

## Slot arc (fixed for the first clip)

| Slot | Beat | Default camera |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PACKED — the sealed, taped box on a surface in front of the creator, fingers already at the tape; product not visible | static, medium |
| 2 | REVEAL — product just out, lifted toward the chest; box at the frame edge or gone; the honest peak | static, medium close |
| 3 | FOCUS — product held toward the lens, product dominates the frame; box gone | static, tight / macro |
| 4 | SATISFACTION — settled, product in hand, warm ownership beat | selfie, medium close |

## Box logic

- Small or medium items open on a table — a clean, styled surface matching the room
  (light wood, marble, white lacquer). Only genuinely large items (furniture, bikes) go
  on the floor. Never a workbench, garage, folding table or cluttered surface.
- The box rests on the surface; nobody lifts or carries it.
- Generic box only: when open in slot 2, crumpled tissue in a colour that suits the
  product peeks out. With a real package photo, show that package's own opening (lid,
  drawer, sleeve) and no added tissue.
- After slot 2 the box never returns; nothing is re-packed or re-taped. Exactly one box.

## Board and clip specifics

- Board references: product → creator → package photo (if any).
- Clip references: cleaned board → creator → product.
- Part 2+ of a series continues after the reveal (first use, a close study of details,
  a show-off moment) — the box never comes back.
- No generated text.
