# Format: try-on

A creator wears one garment, shoe, bag or piece of jewellery and shows fit, drape and
texture. Board: **8 slots** (4 for clips under 8 s) → one clip with matching internal hard
cuts.

## Extra gate

Ordinary clothing and accessories only, shown as a fit or style demo. Refuse lingerie,
underwear, swimwear styled sexually, sheer or transparent garments, fetish wear, nudity
or any emphasis on intimate anatomy — and do not swap in a different outfit to make the
request fit. Adults only, as in the main gate.

## Intake extras

- Product (required, a clear photo of the item), creator photo or gender, duration.
- Approved claims, location, look, language, accent.

## Script

A small personal-want story ("I wanted something that…" only when the user is the
creator; otherwise host framing). No call to action at the end — it ends on the last
spoken beat.

## Slot arc

| Slot | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | PRE-WEAR — the creator in a muted everyday outfit, one plain kraft shopping bag resting nearby |
| 2 | WEARING — the item is on; the bag never appears again |
| 3 | FRONT — a confident front-on stance |
| 4 | TEXTURE — macro of the fabric or material, no hand touching it |
| 5 | TURN — a turn showing the back or profile |
| 6 | DETAIL — macro of one detail (stitching, clasp, sole), no hand touching it |
| 7 | STYLE — styled pose in the room |
| 8 | FINAL LOOK — wide, settled, loop-ready |

With 4 slots: pre-wear → wearing (front) → texture macro → final look.

## Format rules

- The change happens on the hard cut between slots 1 and 2. Never show undressing, a
  costume change, opening the bag or taking the item out of it.
- Slots 4 and 6 are hands-free macros; in the clip they are voiceover beats with the
  mouth not visible. The other beats are lip-synced to camera.
- No mirrors or reflections anywhere (fit checks love mirrors — refuse them in prompts).
- Lock hair, face, and the item's silhouette, colour, print and design across slots;
  the fit is realistic for the creator's build.
- Part 2+ of a series moves to another room of the same home, then outdoors, keeping
  the item and the creator identical.
- Board references: product → creator. Clip references: cleaned board → creator →
  product. No generated text.
