# Photography vocabulary

Vague words give generic images. Every prompt names the light, the lens and the surface
in the terms a commercial photographer would use. Pick from the lists below.

## Light — direction (always state one)

- key 45° camera-left / 45° camera-right — the classic three-quarter key
- frontal, flat — even e-commerce and beauty fill
- overhead beauty dish — top-down sculpted beauty light
- three-quarter back rim — separates the subject from the backdrop
- low side raking — reveals texture and grain
- butterfly from above the lens — glamour light with a small shadow under the nose or cap
- Rembrandt key high at 45° — portrait light with a triangle on the shadow cheek
- fill from below the lens — lifts shadows under the chin or base

## Light — quality (always state one)

- large diffused softbox — broad, wrapping, gentle
- bare hard strobe — crisp shadow edges
- flagged / gobo-shaped — light cut into a controlled shape
- gridded spot — tight pool of light
- bounced fill — soft secondary from a card or reflector
- open skylight — even daylight with no direct sun
- dappled through leaves — broken natural pattern
- motivated practical — a lamp, candle or screen visible in the scene

## Color temperature (always a Kelvin number)

| Source | Kelvin | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Candle | 2000–2800K | Intimate, warm |
| Tungsten lamp | 3000–3400K | Cozy home |
| Warm mixed interior | 3500–4000K | Lived-in |
| Neutral mixed interior | 4000–4500K | Everyday interior |
| Studio daylight | 5000–5500K | Neutral standard |
| Overcast | 6000–6500K | Cool, soft |
| Shade / blue hour | 7000K+ | Cool, dramatic |

Write "4500K window light mixed with a warm lamp", not "warm light".

## Lens and camera

**Focal length (always one):** 24mm wide (environmental, slight stretch) · 35mm
(documentary, lifestyle) · 50mm (neutral, eye-like) · 85mm (flattering compression) ·
100mm macro (fine detail) · 135mm (strong isolation).

**Aperture (always one):** f/1.4 (very shallow, soft bokeh) · f/1.8–f/2 (portrait
separation) · f/2.8 (moderately shallow) · f/4–f/5.6 (subject and context both
readable) · f/8–f/11 (everything sharp, packshot standard) · f/16 (maximum depth).

**Depth of field phrasing:** "shallow depth of field, creamy defocused background" ·
"medium depth separating product from setting" · "deep focus, sharp front to back".

## Surfaces

seamless paper sweep · linen · raw oak or walnut grain · polished marble · board-formed
concrete · brushed steel or aluminium · velvet · worn leather · aged paper · glazed
ceramic · woven jute or sisal · terrazzo · travertine · frosted acrylic · still water.

**Texture cues:** tack-sharp surface detail · visible skin pores · individual hair
strands · condensation beads on glass · dust motes catching light · real fabric weave ·
fine scratches on metal.

## Shadows (always say how they fall)

- soft falloff with a smooth gradient
- crisp shadow from a hard source
- contact shadow at the base only, clean backdrop
- broken dappled shadow from foliage
- deep shadow side with a touch of fill
- gentle wraparound from a large source

## Palette phrasing

Name actual colors, two or three of them:

- warm earth — terracotta, ochre, dried sage
- cool neutral — bone, soft grey, deep navy
- monochrome green — forest through moss
- cream with an oxblood accent
- pastel trio — dusty rose, sage, butter
- black with a single saturated red

## Atmosphere

light shafts through dust · steam rising from a cup · window light through a sheer
curtain · low golden backlight with a lens flare · morning haze · candle underglow ·
flat overcast calm.

## Quality markers (close the prompt with a few)

editorial commercial finish · tack-sharp · hyper-detailed materials · photorealistic ·
professional retouching standard · magazine-cover quality.

## Words to replace

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| good lighting | direction + quality + Kelvin |
| nice background | the surface, its color, its gradient |
| beautiful product | the shot type, angle and framing |
| professional photo | the commercial register plus a lens |
| high quality | tack-sharp, hyper-detailed |
| realistic | photorealistic, no AI artifacts |
| modern style | the actual palette, materials and layout |
