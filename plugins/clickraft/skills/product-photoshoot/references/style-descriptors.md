# Style descriptors

A library of photographic looks, described purely by craft: light, palette, composition
and surface. Each mode file names 2–3 keys per preset. Pull the descriptor phrases from
those entries into the prompt's `[STYLE REFERENCE]` section, merged into one or two
sentences.

## Rules

- Keys (the `kebab-case` headings) are internal lookup labels. Never put a key into a
  prompt, a question or a delivery message — write out the descriptors instead.
- Never add a person, studio, publication, retailer or brand name to a prompt, even if
  the user mentions one. Translate a named influence into the matching descriptors here.
- Combine entries from the same family (see Compatibility). Mixing families produces
  muddled results.

## Still life — clean and graphic

### `graphic-color-block`
- Light: flat, even, shadow-controlled studio light; crisp but soft-edged contact shadows.
- Palette: two or three solid, confident colors, cleanly separated.
- Composition: geometric order, precise spacing, objects aligned on a grid or arranged
  in repeating patterns; generous breathing room.
- Surface: matte seamless paper, painted plinths, smooth laminates.

### `playful-pop-still-life`
- Light: hard, bright direct light or flash with a sharp cast shadow.
- Palette: saturated, cheerful, high-contrast pairings.
- Composition: witty, slightly absurd staging; objects tilted, stacked or in odd company.
- Surface: glossy colored backdrops, candy-bright props.

### `soft-surreal-color`
- Light: diffuse glow with gentle gradients across the backdrop.
- Palette: saturated but velvety hues blending into each other.
- Composition: simple, dreamlike object placement, a hint of the impossible.
- Surface: smooth gradient sweeps, frosted acrylic, satin.

### `handmade-craft-conceptual`
- Light: soft directional key that reveals edges and folds.
- Palette: clean paper tones with one or two bright accents.
- Composition: product framed by handmade set pieces — cut paper, folded card, models.
- Surface: paper, card stock, tactile handmade textures.

## Still life — dramatic and premium

### `classical-still-life`
- Light: single soft north-window-style source, deep gentle falloff, rich shadow.
- Palette: restrained, desaturated, timeless tones.
- Composition: quiet, centered or slightly offset, museum-like stillness.
- Surface: aged wood, stone, plain plaster backdrops.

### `sculpted-dramatic-light`
- Light: carved, high-contrast lighting with gridded spots and strong rim; light treated
  as a sculpting tool.
- Palette: deep blacks against luminous highlights, one jewel-tone accent.
- Composition: bold, isolating the product against darkness.
- Surface: glossy black, polished stone, liquid reflections.

### `high-concept-premium`
- Light: precise, controlled studio light that makes impossible staging look real.
- Palette: polished, refined, usually limited to two or three hues.
- Composition: a single surreal idea executed with technical perfection.
- Surface: built sets, mirrors, water, sculpted props.

### `monolithic-color-sculpture`
- Light: strong single key with clean architectural shadow.
- Palette: one dominant saturated color, monochrome variations of it.
- Composition: sculptural, monumental arrangements; product as part of a form.
- Surface: color-matched blocks, plinths, poured resin.

### `surreal-levitation`
- Light: clean soft key from above-front; soft shadow beneath a floating object.
- Palette: minimal neutrals or one bold backdrop color.
- Composition: unexpected juxtapositions, objects floating or balanced impossibly.
- Surface: seamless sweeps, glossy floors, invisible supports.

## Lifestyle, food and home

### `warm-editorial-table`
- Light: soft daylight from a window at the side, warm 4000–5000K, natural falloff.
- Palette: warm creams, natural wood, appetizing reds and greens.
- Composition: overhead or 45° table scenes, styled but lived-in, crumbs and hands allowed.
- Surface: linen, worn wood, ceramic plates.

### `sunlit-airy-lifestyle`
- Light: bright, airy, lifted shadows, gentle warmth.
- Palette: soft, light, sun-washed naturals.
- Composition: relaxed, candid moments, easy negative space.
- Surface: light woods, white ceramic, fresh greenery.

### `handcrafted-moody-warm`
- Light: low-key natural light from one small window, dark corners, warm glow.
- Palette: earthy browns, deep greens, cream highlights.
- Composition: intimate, rustic still lifes with seasonal props.
- Surface: weathered wood, linen, stoneware, dried botanicals.

### `editorial-interiors`
- Light: balanced daylight mixed with warm practical lamps.
- Palette: considered, designer neutrals with one accent color.
- Composition: architectural lines, layered rooms, objects in context.
- Surface: plaster walls, upholstery, natural stone, wood floors.

### `nordic-soft-daylight`
- Light: cool soft daylight, very low contrast, long gentle shadows.
- Palette: whites, pale greys, raw birch, a black accent.
- Composition: restrained, uncluttered, functional.
- Surface: pale wood, linen, matte ceramic.

### `bright-modern-food`
- Light: high-key, crisp, clean daylight.
- Palette: vivid fresh produce colors against white or pastel.
- Composition: graphic overhead layouts, clean edges.
- Surface: white stone, pastel boards.

### `color-sorted-flatlay`
- Light: even overhead diffusion, almost shadowless.
- Palette: objects ordered by hue into gradients or rainbows.
- Composition: top-down, grid or gradient arrangements, nothing overlapping.
- Surface: plain matte board.

## Beauty

### `glossy-beauty`
- Light: beauty dish or ring key, sculpted cheekbones, specular highlights on lips and glass.
- Palette: rich, glossy, high-impact color.
- Composition: tight crops, flawless styling that still keeps real skin texture.
- Surface: lacquered, wet-look, mirror-polished.

### `modern-minimal-beauty`
- Light: soft frontal light, natural skin, little contouring.
- Palette: muted skin tones, soft neutrals.
- Composition: understated close-ups, quiet expressions, honest texture.
- Surface: bare skin, matte backdrops.

### `skin-sculpting-light`
- Light: directional light that shapes skin like sculpture, strong highlight roll-off.
- Palette: luminous skin against deep or pure backgrounds.
- Composition: abstracted body parts, graphic crops.
- Surface: dewy skin, glass, liquid.

### `experimental-beauty`
- Light: inventive, sometimes colored or gelled light.
- Palette: unexpected color accents on neutral bases.
- Composition: conceptual, playful crops and props.
- Surface: mixed textures, reflective elements.

## Fashion

### `whimsical-fantasy`
- Light: painterly, often warm, theatrical.
- Palette: rich storybook colors.
- Composition: fairy-tale scale play, oversized props, elaborate sets.
- Surface: fabrics, flowers, handmade set builds.

### `glossy-high-fashion`
- Light: polished studio light, strong key with perfect fill, glossy skin.
- Palette: saturated, luxurious.
- Composition: powerful poses, bold styling, high polish.
- Surface: seamless studio, lacquer, metallics.

### `dramatic-editorial`
- Light: cinematic, bold contrast, sometimes hard and directional.
- Palette: deep, dramatic, sometimes desaturated.
- Composition: strong attitude, sweeping poses, magazine-cover tension.
- Surface: studio or opulent interiors.

### `dark-moody-fashion`
- Light: hard light, deep shadows, low-key.
- Palette: blacks, deep reds, cold tones.
- Composition: tense, provocative framing.
- Surface: concrete, leather, night interiors.

### `narrative-portrait`
- Light: painterly window or set light that builds a story.
- Palette: rich but natural.
- Composition: subject in a meaningful environment, story in the frame.
- Surface: real locations, props that carry meaning.

### `natural-confident`
- Light: sunlit, warm, casual flare.
- Palette: natural, warm, healthy tones.
- Composition: candid, energetic, joyful movement.
- Surface: outdoor locations, denim, cotton, sand.

### `street-candid`
- Light: available daylight, sometimes reflector fill.
- Palette: real city colors.
- Composition: caught-in-motion, slightly long lens, background bustle blurred.
- Surface: pavement, brick, glass storefronts.

## Outdoor and atmosphere

### `atmospheric-documentary`
- Light: soft overcast or low sun, quiet and gentle.
- Palette: muted, slightly faded colors, visible film grain.
- Composition: observational, unforced, honest moments.
- Surface: real landscapes and towns.

### `youthful-free-outdoor`
- Light: backlit sun, flare, golden haze.
- Palette: warm, saturated summer tones.
- Composition: movement, freedom, bodies in nature.
- Surface: fields, water, open sky.

### `environmental-landscape`
- Light: natural, time-of-day driven.
- Palette: landscape-led, earthy.
- Composition: small subject inside a large environment, strong sense of place.
- Surface: forests, mountains, coastlines.

### `epic-monochrome`
- Light: dramatic natural light, strong skies.
- Palette: rich black-and-white tonality.
- Composition: grand scale, deep depth of field.
- Surface: vast landscapes, weathered textures.

### `cinematic-staged-narrative`
- Light: controlled, film-set lighting with motivated practicals, twilight blue mixed
  with warm interiors.
- Palette: painterly, slightly desaturated with warm pools of light.
- Composition: frozen narrative moment, deliberate symmetry or tableau.
- Surface: staged rooms, suburban exteriors.

## Mood, retro and conceptual

### `pastel-nostalgic`
- Light: soft, hazy, slightly overexposed.
- Palette: pastels — pinks, lilacs, mint.
- Composition: intimate, dreamy, youth-culture feel.
- Surface: bedrooms, tulle, glitter, soft fabrics.

### `raw-flash-retro`
- Light: direct on-camera-style flash, hard shadows, intentionally unpolished.
- Palette: punchy, slightly off color.
- Composition: casual, snapshot-like, irreverent.
- Surface: everyday real environments.

### `abstract-editorial`
- Light: experimental light play, abstract gradients.
- Palette: minimal, sometimes a single color field.
- Composition: abstract crops, color and light as subject.
- Surface: paper, fabric, light leaks.

### `surreal-conceptual`
- Light: clean studio light that keeps the absurd believable.
- Palette: bold, sometimes clashing on purpose.
- Composition: bodies and objects arranged in odd conceptual ways.
- Surface: studio sweeps, unexpected materials.

## Portrait and tech

### `tech-modern-editorial`
- Light: crisp, cool, controlled studio light with clean rim.
- Palette: cool neutrals, one bright accent.
- Composition: precise, graphic, minimal.
- Surface: glass, brushed aluminium, matte black.

### `sharp-modern-portrait`
- Light: punchy, contrasty, clean.
- Palette: bold background color with neutral wardrobe.
- Composition: direct, confident, slightly graphic.
- Surface: seamless colored paper.

### `monochrome-editorial-portrait`
- Light: soft, natural, timeless black-and-white.
- Palette: monochrome, rich mid-tones.
- Composition: honest, unretouched close portraits.
- Surface: plain walls, natural settings.

## Compatibility

**Good families** (pick 2–3 from one family):

- Clean product: `graphic-color-block` + `surreal-levitation` + `playful-pop-still-life`
- Glossy beauty: `skin-sculpting-light` + `glossy-beauty` + `modern-minimal-beauty`
- Warm food and lifestyle: `warm-editorial-table` + `sunlit-airy-lifestyle` + `bright-modern-food`
- High editorial fashion: `whimsical-fantasy` + `glossy-high-fashion` + `dramatic-editorial`
- Handcrafted lifestyle: `handcrafted-moody-warm` + `editorial-interiors` + `nordic-soft-daylight`
- Tech modern: `tech-modern-editorial` + `surreal-levitation` + `sharp-modern-portrait`
- Premium conceptual: `high-concept-premium` + `surreal-levitation` + `monolithic-color-sculpture`

**Avoid mixing:** surreal with clean-corporate, high fashion with corporate, dark moody
with bright lifestyle.

## Turning entries into a prompt line

Merge, deduplicate, keep it under ~40 words. Example from `graphic-color-block` +
`surreal-levitation`:

> Flat, even studio light with soft contact shadows, two solid confident colors, precise
> geometric spacing and generous breathing room, one gently impossible detail. Commercial
> still life, editorial quality.
